ABOUT NOVA OS

What is Nova OS?

Nova OS is a modern open-source operating system focused on freedom, transparency and performance. It is built to be a platform where developers can learn, experiment and create without unnecessary limitations.

  • Linux-based and modular architecture
  • Developer and power-user focused
  • Privacy, security and transparency at its core
  • Open development and community contribution

ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

NOVA OS Kernel

The core operating-system repository is taking shape: a freestanding x86_64 kernel, Limine boot path, serial diagnostics and an early framebuffer display. Build and QEMU boot validation are still in progress; this is an experimental milestone, not a release-ready OS.

Aurum Gold Bot

A demo-first XAU/USD research dashboard for MetaTrader 5, with historical candle backtesting and a read-only market-data bridge in development. The goal is to research strategy behaviour without placing trades.

FREE · BROWSER-NATIVE

NOVA Video Studio

A browser-based video creation experiment for animated scenes, cinematic titles and motion graphics. Includes landscape, portrait and square layouts, live preview and WebM export; it is not a photorealistic text-to-video model.
