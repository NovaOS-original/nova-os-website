Use supported Hugging Face Inference Providers for text-to-video, image-to-video, or image-plus-prompt video generation. This supplements NOVA's free browser motion-graphics editor and local Windows model runner.

Free does not mean unlimited. Hugging Face lists a small monthly free inference credit (currently US$0.10 for free users, subject to change). Video generation can consume credits quickly or be unavailable for a selected model/provider. You need your own Hugging Face token with Inference Providers permission. Never paste a token into public code or share it.
